    How OceanWatch works

    1. Sentinel-1 SAR finds dark sea-surface patches (possible oil), day or night, through cloud.
    2. Sentinel-2 optical finds spectrally anomalous floating patches (possible debris) in clear weather.
    3. Each candidate gets a confidence score and look-alike warnings (low wind, seaweed, sub-pixel mixing, cloud).
    4. An analyst confirms, rejects or requests a field check. Nothing is a confirmed pollutant until then.

    Demo data is synthetic and the detector is a rule-based baseline; no trained model is used yet.
